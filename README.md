# TBPHP — Microneedling Pen & Derma Pen OEM/ODM Manufacturer

**TBPHP** (Guangzhou Xingyun E-Commerce Co., Ltd.) is a Guangzhou-based OEM/ODM manufacturer of [microneedling pen](https://tbphp.shop/) devices and [derma pen](https://tbphp.shop/) systems.

## About

TBPHP designs and manufactures electric microneedling pens, derma pens, and individually packed needle cartridges for clinics, medispas, and private-label brands. The company supports OEM and ODM production, custom branding and packaging, and bilingual documentation.

- 15 years of experience
- Monthly capacity: 50,000 pens and 500,000 cartridges
- CE marked, UKCA documented, and RoHS compliant
- EO-sterilized cartridges per ISO 11135
